This Privacy Policy describes how DKSS Legal collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our website, including:

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you provide to us directly, such as:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Company name, job title, or professional details
  • Information you provide when requesting legal consultation
  • Payment details for legal services
  • Any additional information voluntarily submitted

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and version
  • Device and operating system details
  • Pages visited on our website
  • Referring website or search source

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide legal consultation and related services
  • To respond to client inquiries and communication
  • To process billing and service-related transactions
  • To personalize user experience on our website
  • To improve website performance and service delivery
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Trusted service providers (e.g., hosting, payment processing, analytics)
  • Legal authorities, if required by law
  • New business entities in the event of merger/acquisition

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to improve website functionality and user experience. You may disable cookies via your browser settings, but certain features may not function properly.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information, but no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute protection of data.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to request correction or update
  • Right to request deletion, where applicable
  • Right to object to certain data processing activities

7. Data Retention

We retain information as long as necessary for legal, professional, or business requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under age 18.

9. Third-Party Links

External sites linked from our website are not governed by this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Please review it regularly.

11. Attorney-Client Privilege

Contacting us through this website does not create an attorney-client relationship. Confidential matters require a direct consultation.
