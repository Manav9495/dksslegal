Overview

Our Trademark practice ensures brand protection through comprehensive trademark legal services, including trademark registration, opposition, prosecution, renewal, enforcement, and infringement litigation. We assist individuals, startups, SMEs, and corporations in securing and defending their brand identity.

Our team provides end-to-end support from name/logo selection and search reports to filing, responding to objections, and legal action against unauthorized use of your brand. We also represent clients before the Trademark Registry, IPAB/High Courts, and other legal forums.

Our Trademark Services

  • Trademark Registration (Name, Logo, Tagline, Packaging Design)
  • Trademark Search, Analysis & Class Identification
  • Reply to Trademark Objections (Examination Reports)
  • Trademark Opposition & Counter-Statements
  • Trademark Rectification & Cancellation Proceedings
  • Trademark Licensing, Assignment & Transfer Agreements
  • Trademark Renewal & Maintenance Support
  • Trademark Watch & Brand Monitoring Services
  • Infringement Actions & Cease & Desist Notice Drafting
  • Representation Before Trademark Registry & High Courts
