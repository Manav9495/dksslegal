Overview

Our Arbitration practice provides comprehensive legal services for dispute resolution through arbitration proceedings. We represent clients in domestic and international arbitrations, offering strategic counsel that helps resolve conflicts efficiently while protecting our clients' interests.

With deep expertise in arbitration law, procedural rules, and enforcement of arbitral awards, our team is equipped to handle complex commercial disputes across various sectors including construction, infrastructure, corporate disputes, and international trade.

Our Arbitration Services

  • Drafting & Responding to Arbitration Notices
  • Representation Before Arbitrators & Arbitral Tribunals
  • Section 9 & Section 34 Petitions under Arbitration and Conciliation Act
  • Enforcement & Execution of Arbitral Awards
  • Settlement Negotiation & Mediation Support
  • International Commercial Arbitration
  • Domestic Institutional Arbitration
  • Emergency Arbitrator Proceedings
  • Challenging Arbitral Awards
  • Arbitration Agreement Drafting and Review

Why Choose Our Arbitration Practice

Case Study

Successful Resolution of Construction Dispute

We recently represented a major infrastructure developer in a complex construction arbitration involving claims exceeding ₹50 crores. Our team successfully navigated the arbitration process, presented compelling evidence, and secured a favorable award for our client.

The arbitration resulted in our client recovering substantial amounts while avoiding prolonged litigation in civil courts, demonstrating the effectiveness of arbitration as a dispute resolution mechanism.
