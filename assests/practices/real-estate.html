Overview

Our Real Estate Law practice offers comprehensive legal support for individuals, builders, developers, investors, landlords, tenants, and corporate entities involved in property transactions and disputes. We assist in drafting, vetting, due diligence, regulatory compliance, and litigation related to real estate matters.

We handle matters involving RERA, land acquisition, title investigations, joint development agreements, registry disputes, property inheritance, commercial leasing, and builder-buyer conflicts. Our objective is to ensure secure, transparent, and legally compliant property dealings.

Our Real Estate Legal Services

  • Title Verification & Property Due Diligence
  • Drafting & Registration of Sale Deeds and Lease Agreements
  • Builder-Buyer Dispute Resolution (RERA / NCLT / Consumer Court)
  • Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA) Compliance & Representation
  • Property Partition, Inheritance & Family Settlement Disputes
  • Joint Development & Collaboration Agreements
  • Commercial Leasing & Rent Agreements
  • Land Acquisition, Conversion & Mutation Procedures
  • Disputes related to Property Fraud, Cheating & Misrepresentation
