Overview

Our Cyber Crime legal team provides strong legal support for victims and accused involved in cyber offences. As digital threats grow, we help individuals and businesses protect their identity, reputation, digital assets, and data integrity by providing specialized cyber law guidance and representation.

From online frauds, data theft, defamation, harassment, phishing, hacking, impersonation, online blackmail, to financial scams and social media abuse — we ensure swift legal action and representation before Cyber Cells and Courts.

Our Cyber Crime Services

  • Filing Complaints for Cyber Fraud, Identity Theft & Online Scams
  • Legal Action in Cases of Hacking, Phishing, Data Breach & Malware Attacks
  • Defamation, Harassment & Blackmail on Social Media
  • Representation Before Cyber Cells, Police & Courts
  • Data Privacy, Cyber Security & IT Act Compliance Advisory
  • Digital Evidence Preservation, Collection & Forensics Guidance
  • Drafting Website Policies, NDAs & Technology & Software Contracts
  • Cyber Bullying & Online Threat Complaints
  • Corporate Cyber Risk & Incident Response Support

Protection Against Social Media Blackmail
