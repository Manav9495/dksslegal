Overview

We provide comprehensive legal representation in matters relating to insolvency, loan defaults, banking disputes, financial recovery actions, and corporate restructuring under the Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC), SARFAESI Act, RDDBFI Act, and other financial laws.

Our firm represents Financial Creditors, Operational Creditors, Corporate Debtors, Banks, NBFCs, and Individual Borrowers before Tribunals and Courts across India including NCLT, NCLAT, DRT, and High Courts.

Our Legal Services

  • IBC Proceedings under Sections 7, 9 & 10
  • Representation before NCLT & NCLAT
  • Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP)
  • Debt Recovery Proceedings before DRT
  • SARFAESI Notices, Objections & Auctions
  • Loan Restructuring & Settlement Negotiations
  • Banking Fraud & Financial Misconduct Disputes
  • IBC Liquidation, Claims & Committee of Creditors Support
  • Writs & Appeals before High Courts & Supreme Court
  • Drafting & Reviewing Financial Contracts & Loan Agreements
