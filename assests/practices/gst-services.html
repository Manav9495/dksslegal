Overview

Our GST practice provides end-to-end legal and compliance support under the Goods and Services Tax regime. We assist businesses in navigating complex indirect tax regulations, ensuring accurate compliance, efficient tax planning, and effective resolution of disputes before GST authorities and appellate forums.

With strong domain knowledge and experience across various industries, our GST team ensures proactive advisory, audit support, litigation management, and representation in departmental investigations, show cause notices, and appeals.

Our GST Services

  • GST Registration & Compliance Advisory
  • Filing of GST Returns, Reconciliations & Annual Returns
  • GST Audit Support & Documentation
  • Handling GST Show Cause Notices & Summons
  • Appeals before Appellate Authorities & Tribunals
  • Input Tax Credit (ITC) Claim & Dispute Resolution
  • Tax Planning Strategies for Businesses
  • Representation during GST Investigations & Hearings
  • Drafting & Review of GST Agreements and Policies
  • Industry-specific Indirect Tax Advisory
